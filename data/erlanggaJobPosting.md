## 📋 Responsibilities & Qualifications (Verbatim)

1. Minimum Bachelor's or Master's degree (S1/S2) in Information Technology, Information Systems, Computer Science, or a related field, with a minimum GPA of 3.00.
2. Possess 5–10 years of professional experience in the fields of Information Technology, Information Systems, or Computer Science.
3. Maximum age 40 years (from official graphic flyer).
4. Excellent communication skills.
5. Leadership, Management, Communication, Public Speaking.
6. IT Infrastructure: GCP, AWS, DataCenters, Security & AV, Backup Management.
7. Database, SDLC, ERP (Dynamics), CRM (Qontak), Auto ML, Data Science, PowerBI, Db Architecture, Programming, HRMS (Darwinbox).
8. Have an understanding of the tools that support work at Erlangga Group.
9. Have the ability to direct in accordance with a systematic workflow and convince users about changes due to the implementation of a computerized system.
10. Have the ability to deal with emergencies.
11. Good project management skills to validate estimates and carry out capacity planning.
12. Performing analysis, planning and design of incoming IT requests.
13. Planning and budgeting IT DevOps & SecOps for the next 1 year each year.
14. Manage IT DevOps & SecOps requests according to company needs and prioritize. Ensure that every plan is in accordance with the company's strategy.
15. Manage IT infrastructures & applications that are already running and develop according to the latest technology.
16. Coordinate IT-related tasks and operations with all regional IT branches.
17. Ensure all existing IT infrastructures & applications are fully documented.
